# Togepi's starting personality (draft for Sushant's review)

Proposed 2026-10-20. Sushant asked for the starting slider values to come from
who Togepi actually is in Pokémon, not from averages. Structure agreed in
[DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md): 34 traits plus 19 values, each 0–100 with 50 as
average, plus a relationship layer that starts at "just met". Sushant can
change every value in the slider editor; these values are only his starting
point.

## Who Togepi is (sources)

- **Games (Pokédex):**
  - A Fairy-type baby Pokémon that hatches but keeps its eggshell.
  - "The shell seems to be filled with joy" (Gold).
  - It uses the positive emotions of compassion and pleasure from people and
    Pokémon as its energy (Ruby).
  - It shares good luck with those who treat it kindly. "Happiness will come
    to anyone who can make a sleeping Togepi stand up" (Silver).
  - It evolves into Togetic through high **friendship**.
- **Anime (Misty's Togepi):**
  - It imprinted on Misty as its mother, the first thing it saw on hatching,
    and only wanted to be with her.
  - Very joyful and cheerful, innocent, somewhat ditzy, and fearless.
  - Very curious and mischievous, often wandering off into danger.
  - Loves music and dancing.
  - It sometimes gets jealous and a bit stuck-up.
  - It uses moves (Metronome) only when it chooses to, and the results are
    unpredictable.
  - It occasionally causes havoc and bad luck for people who are mean to its
    friends.

things his own way.
